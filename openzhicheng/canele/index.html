The Canelé Recipe

Canelés de Bordeaux — a crackly, bittersweet caramelized shell around a soft custard crumb. Not secret, just good.

Plan ahead: the batter must rest 24–48 hours in the fridge. Day 1 — make the batter (20 min). Day 2 or 3 — bake (about 1 hour).

Ingredients (about 12 canelés)

  • Whole milk500 g
  • Unsalted butter50 g
  • Vanilla bean, split (or 2 tsp extract)1
  • All-purpose flour100 g
  • Granulated sugar220 g
  • Fine salt1 pinch
  • Whole egg1
  • Egg yolks2
  • Dark rum50 g
  • Beeswax + butter for the molds (or just butter)as needed

Method

  1. Warm the milk, butter, and vanilla bean to a bare simmer, then take it off the heat and let it cool until just warm (about 60 °C). Hot milk scrambles the eggs; that's the main way this recipe fails.
  2. Whisk the flour, sugar, and salt in a bowl. In another bowl, whisk the egg and yolks together.
  3. Pour the eggs into the dry mix, then stream in the warm milk while whisking gently — you want a thin, crêpe-like batter with as little air as possible. Stir in the rum.
  4. Strain the batter, cover, and refrigerate 24–48 hours. The rest hydrates the flour and relaxes the gluten; skipping it gives you cakey, pale canelés.
  5. Baking day: heat the oven to 240 °C. Coat the molds with a thin film of melted beeswax + butter (50/50) if using copper, or softened butter for silicone. Chill the coated molds.
  6. Stir the cold batter gently (it separates), and fill the molds to about 1 cm from the rim. Cold batter into a hot oven is what builds the crust.
  7. Bake 15 minutes at 240 °C, then drop to 180 °C and bake another 45–60 minutes, until the tops are deep mahogany — darker than feels safe. Pale canelés taste of sweet nothing.
  8. Unmold immediately onto a rack, and wait 30 minutes: the crust sets as it cools. Best within 6 hours of baking.

Notes

Molds. Copper gives the best crust but demands the beeswax coating and careful seasoning. Silicone works — expect a slightly softer shell and bake on a preheated metal tray to compensate.

Doming. If they puff out of the molds mid-bake ("white ass" canelés), pull the tray out, tap them down, and return — or bake 10 °C lower next time.

Rum. It's aroma, not decoration. Substituting is legal; omitting is a misdemeanor.
